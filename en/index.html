▸ How to play ◂

  1. 1. Draft 6 Pokémon: pick 1 card out of 3, six times.
  2. 2. Prepare your team: nature, EVs, ability, moves.
  3. 3. Climb the Battle Tower, floor by floor.
  4. 4. Every 5 floors, face a Mini-Boss.
  5. 5. Every 10 floors, beat a Type Master to earn their Badge.
  6. 6. Watch out for surprises: double battles and events between floors.
  7. 7. After each Boss and Mini-Boss, stop at the Camp: Pokémon Center, Poké Mart and Ranch.
  8. 8. Sometimes a rare visitor sets up at the Camp: the Traveling Merchant or the Safari.
  9. 9. Earn affinity candies by beating trainers and catching Pokémon at the Safari.
  10. 10. Give your candies to the Professor to see your favorite Pokémon more often in the draft.
  11. 11. Every defeat earns Tower Tokens based on your progress, to spend on Starting Boosts.

A free Pokémon roguelike, no download needed

Draft Mon is a Pokémon fan game you play online, right in your browser, on computer or phone. Every run is different: the draft gives you an unexpected team, and the Battle Tower gets harder with every floor.

Over 1000 Pokémon from Kanto to Paldea, Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, Dynamax and Terastallization, three difficulty levels and a Team Builder compatible with Pokémon Showdown. No account, no ads.

Draft Mon is still in beta: the logo and banner are temporary AI-generated images. Are you an artist who'd like to offer your services? Drop by the Discord.
